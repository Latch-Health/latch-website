Breastfeeding, designed around you.

Latch is a maternal-health company building personalized, comfortable, and intuitive support for breastfeeding and pumping — starting in pregnancy and adapting all through postpartum.

We're pro-breastfeeding and pro-Fed is Best: however you feed your baby — breastfeeding, pumping, combination, or formula — you deserve support that meets you there.

Where we are today

Our first pilot starts in January 2027 with a small group of families due mid‑February to April 2027, working directly with us and an IBCLC advisor. It builds on a prototype feedback round with Vermont WIC families in fall 2026. We're finalizing pricing now, with a plan to keep WIC and Medicaid access discounted.

Why we started

A NICU, a flange, and a lot of trial and error.

“When our daughter was in the NICU, we were pumping for thirty minutes at a time because no one had told us otherwise — until a nurse finally said that wasn't necessary. We kept doing it that way at home for weeks afterward, stressed and guessing, piecing together guidance from whichever care team member we happened to see that day.”

Around that same time, we were already on our third flange size, and it still didn't fit right. We remember thinking we'd need a paper ruler just to measure again. It felt medieval. Talking with other new parents and lactation professionals made it clear this wasn't a product problem — it was a guidance problem. Parents often encounter inconsistent advice and too much trial and error, whether that's in the NICU or at home. That gap, more than any single gadget, is what Latch is here to close.

How we think

Four things guide every decision.

These aren't taglines — they're the questions we ask before we build anything.

Personalized by nature

Every parent is different, and every feeding path is valid — breastfeeding, pumping, combination, or formula. Support should reflect individual bodies and choices, not a single average case.

Professionals, not replacements

Technology should complement — not replace — the judgment and compassion of IBCLCs, doulas, and nurses.

Trust before technology

We collect as little as possible, keep it on infrastructure we control rather than third-party AI platforms, and require explicit consent for any use of personal images.

Adapt to the parent

Products and support should adapt to the parent — not the other way around — from pregnancy through every stage of feeding.

Privacy

Trust before technology.

We collect as little information as possible, favor on-device processing where it's feasible, and require explicit, informed consent before any personal images are used for anything. Consent shouldn't be buried in fine print — so we try not to bury ours.

  • We're bootstrapped and independent — no investors to answer to — so pricing stays fair, not growth-at-all-costs.
  • No clinical claims, diagnoses, or guarantees — Latch is in pilot, not a finished product.
  • Your information stays on infrastructure we control and is never used to train outside AI models without your explicit, informed permission.

Pricing & access

Parents pay. Nobody gets priced out.

We're bootstrapping Latch on purpose — no investors to answer to, which means pricing stays fair instead of growth-driven. Here's the plan as it stands today.

How it will work

A simple package — or pay monthly instead

  • One package covers you from pregnancy through postpartum — pay it upfront, or spread it out monthly instead. Your choice.
  • Once that active window ends, a lower-cost plan keeps your data saved — there if you have another baby, or if you just want to hold onto it.
  • Exact pricing is still being finalized — we'd rather get it right than rush it.
  • Costs are never passed on to the IBCLCs, doulas, or nurses who work with us.
